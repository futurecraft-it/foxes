# <span id="new-pets"></span> ✨ **New pets**!

### <span id="fox"></span> 🦊 Fox - A cute and cuddly being.
- ❤️ **Taming** - In order to tame a fox, feed them with Raw Chicken. Notice that if you don't want to tame the fox you can use the default "_trusted player_" system.
- ⚔️ **Combat** - Tamed fox will attack any mob that attacks their owner, and they will also attack any mob that their owner attacks or that has been attacked by the owner, player included.
- 🔥 **Comfort Seeker** - Your fox will seek for comfortable (warm) blocks to sleep on like lit furnaces and beds.
- 🧠 **AI** - Once tamed, your fox will gain new behaviors.
  - **Follow** - Your fox will follow you around and teleport if too far away.
  - **Sit** - Your fox will sit and stay in place until you tell it to follow you again.

### <span id="slime"></span>🟩 Slime - A sticky clingy cutie.
- ❤️ **Taming** - In order to tame a slime, feed them with Honey Bottles. Empty bottles will be returned to the player.
- ⚔️ **Combat** - Tamed slime will attack any mob that attacks their owner, and they will also attack any mob that their owner attacks or that has been attacked by the owner, player included.
- 🧠 **AI** - Once tamed, your slime will gain new behaviors.
    - **Follow** - Your slime will follow you around and teleport if too far away.
    - **Sit** - Your slime will sit and stay in place until you tell it to follow you again.
